Privacy Policy

FUKUI SEISAKUSHO CO., LTD. considers protection of privacy as social accountability and endeavor to handle, manage and keep with great care.

Purpose of acquiring personal information

In order to respond inquiries from customer, we will acquire and use personal information, recognize its importance and handle properly.

Use of personal information

We will use only the personal information that is necessary to achieve intended purposes. In case we need to use personal information out of specified scope, we will obtain prior approval of each respective individual.

Provision of personal information to third parties.

We will not disclose any personal information to any third parties without such person’s approval in principal, except following cases:

１. Request from government and public office such as police department.
２. Applicable laws or regulations.

Security

We will maintain the appropriate system for strict management of personal information.

Continuously improvement

We will strive to improve protection of personal information continuously.

Compliance

We will comply with laws, regulations or other rules relevant to protection of personal information.

Revision of privacy policy

We will review and revise the privacy policy appropriately.
Revised privacy policy will be up-dated on our web-site immediately.
