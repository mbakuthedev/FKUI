Top Message

Since FUKUI was founded in 1936, we have supplied safety relief valves to customers in more than 80 countries. Our valves protect equipment in power plants, LNG and gas supply chains, marine cargo systems, and emerging clean-energy applications including hydrogen and carbon capture.

Built on Japanese precision manufacturing and rigorous quality standards, FUKUI products are trusted as final safety devices where reliability is essential. Our UK office in Burwell, Cambridgeshire provides a local point of contact for enquiries, product information and customer support across the United Kingdom and Europe.

As energy markets evolve and technical demands increase, we remain committed to engineering excellence, responsive service, and the safety of the industries and communities we serve. We welcome your enquiries and look forward to working with you.
